# Businessnify

### Launch your online store. Build your business.

Businessnify is a SaaS platform designed to help businesses launch and manage their own branded online stores.

Instead of building an entire e-commerce system from scratch, businesses can get their own storefront and management dashboard through one platform.

**[Visit Businessnify](https://businessnify.com/)** · **[Meet the Founder](https://businessnify.com/founder)**

---

## What Businessnify Offers

- **Branded storefronts** — Give your business its own online shopping experience.
- **Store management** — Manage products, inventory, customers, and orders.
- **Custom domains** — Connect your own domain to your store.
- **Automatic SSL** — Secure supported storefront domains with HTTPS.
- **Multi-tenant architecture** — Run multiple stores on a shared platform with tenant separation.

## Built by a Developer, for Businesses

Businessnify is built with a focus on practical software architecture, automation, and scalable application development.

The goal is simple: reduce the technical complexity of launching and managing an online business.
